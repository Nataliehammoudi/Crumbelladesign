Four easy steps

From bag to bakery style

1

Heat

Preheat the oven to 180 degrees Celsius.

2

Mix

Combine the mix, softened butter and one egg in a bowl until a dough forms.

3

Shape

Drop dough 6 cm apart on an ungreased tray.

4

Bake

Bake until the edges turn golden. Cool 2 minutes before lifting.

Baker's tip: chill the dough for 15 to 20 minutes before baking for softer, thicker cookies.
